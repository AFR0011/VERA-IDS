# Evidence Map

`RESULTS_PROVENANCE.md` is the authoritative claim-to-artifact map.
`outputs/summaries/SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv` maps every copied compact artifact to its
source-relative path and SHA-256 hash. `RESULTS.md` interprets those artifacts;
`ERRATA.md` and `RISK_REGISTER.md` identify evidence that cannot yet support a
publication claim.
